In 2017, I had the honor of being invited by the University of Edinburgh to introduce blockchain technology to their students. At the time, the technology was still largely associated with Bitcoin in the public imagination, and universities were only beginning to treat it as a subject worth teaching in its own right.

I put together a two-part session for the occasion, and I'm happy to share the recordings here for anyone looking for a grounding in the fundamentals.
